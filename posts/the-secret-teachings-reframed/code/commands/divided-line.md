Climb the **Divided Line** on:

> $ARGUMENTS

Formal object: a particular is `x = μ_c + ε` — a Form `μ_c` corrupted by circumstance `ε`, lying on a low-dimensional surface `ℳ^d` (`d ≪ D`). The Line is the ladder of representation.

Ascend the four tiers explicitly, each stripping away circumstance:
1. **Eikasia (appearance).** The raw surface of the case — its particular, incidental details.
2. **Pistis (object).** What recognizable thing this is an instance of.
3. **Dianoia (structure).** The mathematical or relational structure underneath — the variables, constraints, and how they relate.
4. **Noesis (governing relation).** The Form: the general principle this case is one sample of. State it as a claim that holds for the whole class, not just this instance.

Then:
- Identify which tier the problem was *handed* to you at, and which tier it should actually be *solved* at — reasoning on the shadow (eikasia) when the Form (noesis) governs is the classic error.
- Give the answer at the Form level, then descend once to apply it back to the specific case.
- Name what is `ε` (incidental to this instance) versus `μ_c` (essential to the class), so the solution generalizes.
