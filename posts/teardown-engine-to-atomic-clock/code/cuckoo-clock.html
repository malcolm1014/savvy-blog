How the clock works

Going train: a cast weight on a chain pulls the gear train; the anchor escapement lets the escape wheel advance exactly one tooth each time the pendulum swings, so the hands track real time. A longer pendulum beats slower — watch Rate error swing as you move the slider.

Strike train: on the hour a lever trips the second weight. It works two bellows feeding pipes tuned a third apart — the high cu then the low ckoo — once per hour-count, and a cam shoves the bird out the door.
