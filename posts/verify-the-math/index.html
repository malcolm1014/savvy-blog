A Zero-Trust Architecture for the Dollar.

Abstract

This paper is written for readers who evaluate systems by their trusted computing base rather than by their marketing. It makes an unusual request of that audience: apply the same provenance discipline to monetary history that you would apply to a dependency you were about to import. Section 2 exists because most of the popular literature on this subject fails that test badly.

Six findings
  1. The trusted computing base is tiny. Twelve voting members, eight scheduled meetings a year, and an audit regime that explicitly excludes monetary policy from external review.
  2. A quarter-trillion-dollar privilege escalation, no exploit required. A self-defined accounting treatment — the "deferred asset" — turned insolvency into an IOU the institution wrote to itself.
  3. Two of the movement's most quoted lines are forgeries. The Jefferson and Wilson quotations are fabrications. Three genuine ones, from Jefferson, Jackson, and Lindbergh, hold up without help.
  4. Security engineering already solved this, in 1975. Saltzer and Schroeder's principles for protecting computer systems transfer to monetary architecture almost without modification.
  5. The window replaces the committee. A fixed buy/sell price in a commodity basket makes the money supply self-adjusting. No officer or body decides it.
  6. Adversaries make the best auditors. Blind, two-auditor, bond-slashing verification gives short sellers standing that no existing reserve system provides.

1. Threat Model

Every security engineer asks the same first question of a system: what must be trusted, and what happens when that trust is violated? Applied to the dollar, the answer is uncomfortably small. Twelve voting members at a table, eight scheduled meetings a year, operating under a statutory mandate that defines neither of its two objectives numerically, and subject to an audit regime that explicitly excludes monetary policy deliberations and transactions from external review.

That is the entire trusted computing base of the world's reserve currency. It is a committee, and it is unaudited on precisely the dimension that matters most.

1.1 Correcting the folklore first

Before describing the real vulnerability, it is necessary to dispose of the popular one. The claim that the Federal Reserve is a private company extracting profit from the American public does not survive contact with the balance sheet, and repeating it forfeits credibility that this argument cannot spare.

  • Structure. The Board of Governors is a federal agency whose members are nominated by the President and confirmed by the Senate. The twelve regional Reserve Banks are federally chartered corporations whose stock is held by member commercial banks, but that stock cannot be sold, traded, or pledged as collateral, carries no vote over monetary policy, and pays a dividend fixed by statute rather than by earnings.
  • Profits. The Federal Reserve finances its operations from income earned on its portfolio and remits net income to the Treasury. For most of the post-war era this was on the order of sixty to one hundred billion dollars annually flowing to the federal government, not to bankers.

Anyone building a case on the private-company framing has built it on sand. The genuine finding is considerably worse.

1.2 The actual vulnerability: privilege escalation without an exploit

Beginning in September 2022, rising policy rates pushed the interest the Federal Reserve pays on bank reserve balances above the yield on securities it had acquired when rates were near zero. Net income turned negative for the first time in decades. Remittances to the Treasury stopped.

What happened next is the part that should interest this audience. Rather than reporting negative capital, which is what a supervised private institution would be compelled to do, the Federal Reserve recorded the accumulated losses as a deferred asset: a negative liability line item representing future profits that must be retained before remittances to the Treasury can resume. It is, in plain terms, an IOU the institution writes to itself, under an accounting treatment it defined for itself, to describe its own insolvency.

As of mid-2026 the consolidated deferred asset stood at roughly $236 billion. New York Fed projections have remittances suspended for several more years. The Federal Reserve's own estimates have suggested that positive transfers may not resume until 2030 or later. That foregone revenue is not absorbed by the institution. It appears as a higher level of federal debt, eventually sustained by higher taxes or reduced expenditure.

Restate that in the vocabulary of this field. An entity paid its counterparties above its own asset yield, became balance-sheet insolvent by any ordinary measure, and then exercised unilateral authority to define the accounting treatment of its own insolvency in a manner that transferred the shortfall to a third party with no standing to object. No exploit was required. No rule was broken. The capability was in the design.

A quarter of a trillion dollars moved, and the only mechanism involved was the absence of an external constraint. This is not an incident report. It is an architecture review finding.

2. Provenance Discipline: The Forged Quotations

A supply-chain attack on an argument, and the primary sources that don't need one.

A reader who verifies signatures on packages should verify quotations in arguments. The monetary reform literature is one of the most heavily contaminated citation environments in American political writing, and the two most widely circulated quotations in it are forgeries. Any document that reproduces them is announcing that its author did not check.

2.1 Two documented fabrications

The Jefferson forgery. The passage beginning “If the American people ever allow private banks to control the issue of their currency, first by inflation, then by deflation…” is classified as spurious by the Jefferson Papers project at Monticello. It appears nowhere in Jefferson's writings, to Albert Gallatin or otherwise. The editors of Respectfully Quoted note the decisive tell: the words inflation and deflation are not documented in English until after Jefferson's lifetime. The text has been traced in print to at least 1933, where it was circulated by Congressman Wright Patman of Texas.

The Wilson forgery. The passage beginning “I am a most unhappy man. I have unwittingly ruined my country” is a composite fabrication. The surrounding material is assembled from two separate chapters of Wilson's The New Freedom, a distillation of campaign speeches published in 1913 before he signed the Federal Reserve Act. The book does not mention the Federal Reserve System at any point. The opening lines about ruin appear nowhere in it, and no source has ever been produced for them. The Woodrow Wilson Presidential Library rejects the attribution.

Both forgeries share a construction method worth naming, because it recurs: authentic fragments are extracted from unrelated contexts, reassembled without indication of rearrangement, and prefaced with invented material that supplies the desired conclusion. It is a supply-chain attack on an argument. The authentic components pass casual inspection and carry the payload.

2.2 Three that hold up

The case against unconstrained monetary discretion does not need forgeries. It can be made entirely from primary sources.

There is an irony in the Pujo record that this paper declines to hide. The Money Trust investigation found concentrated private control of national credit, and its findings helped generate public support for the Federal Reserve Act, the Sixteenth Amendment, and the Clayton Antitrust Act. The investigation into concentrated financial power produced the institution now criticised for concentrating financial power. Institutional reform has a poor track record of solving the problem it was convened to address, which is an argument for mechanism over stewardship.

3. The Serious Literature Already Exists

This design is not novel in its economics. It is a synthesis of four established research programmes, three of which predate the personal computer. Presenting it as an invention would be both false and unnecessary.

  • Separating money creation from lending. Irving Fisher's 100% Money (1935) and the Chicago Plan set out in A Program for Monetary Reform (Simons, Douglas, Fisher, Graham, King, Whittlesey, 1939) proposed removing deposit creation from commercial banks entirely. The programme was revived at the IMF by Benes and Kumhof (2012).
  • Commodity-basket anchoring. Benjamin Graham, better known for Security Analysis, considered his commodity reserve currency plan his most important work. Storage and Stability (1937) proposed a currency backed by a basket of storable commodities operating as an ever-normal granary. Hayek endorsed the approach in A Commodity Reserve Currency (Economic Journal, 1943), having moved off the gold standard specifically because it had been managed by poorly coordinated central banks. Keynes objected, and the exchange is documented in Frank Graham's Keynes vs. Hayek on a Commodity Reserve Currency (1944). Robert Hall's ANCAP proposal (1982) is the modern descendant.
  • Rules over discretion. Kydland and Prescott established in 1977 why discretionary policy underperforms even when policymakers are competent and benevolent. The public observes the policymaker, anticipates the future incentive to renege, and prices that in; announcements therefore become cheap talk. Rules outperform not because rule-followers are wiser but because rules are credible. The result won the 2004 Nobel and is now the framework in which even opponents of rules make their arguments.
  • Endogenous money. The Bank of England's own 2014 description of money creation in the modern economy establishes that the majority of money in circulation is created by commercial banks in the act of lending. This is the defensible core of the debt-money critique, and it is not a fringe position. It is the central bank's published account of its own system.

The contribution this paper attempts is narrower: to specify the audit and attestation layer that these proposals all assumed and none solved.

4. Design Principles Imported from Security Engineering

Saltzer and Schroeder's 1975 principles for the protection of information in computer systems transfer to monetary architecture almost without modification. The mapping is not decorative; each principle resolves a specific historical failure.

  • Least privilege. The reserve authority holds the minimum capability set required to maintain a peg. Every power beyond that is denied by charter rather than left unexercised by custom. Section 7 enumerates the result.
  • Economy of mechanism. One instrument, not four. The authority operates a price window. It does not additionally set rates, purchase assets, or extend credit, because each additional instrument is an additional surface.
  • Open design. Kerckhoffs applied to policy. Security must not depend on the confidentiality of deliberations. Closed-door committee minutes released with a five-year lag are security through obscurity.
  • Complete mediation. Every unit of currency enters or leaves circulation through the window. There is no privileged issuance path, no emergency channel, and no facility.
  • Separation of privilege. Physical vault access, attestation signing, instrument calibration, and audit assignment are held by four mutually independent parties. No two may share corporate control.
  • Fail-safe defaults. The window's default state is open. Refusal to transact is not a discretionary option but a statutory violation. Contrast the failure mode of the classical gold standard, where the default under stress was suspension of convertibility.

One further borrowing, from Lamport, Shostak and Pease (1982): the audit layer is designed on the assumption that a bounded fraction of participants are actively malicious rather than merely negligent. Systems designed for negligence fail against adversaries. Systems designed for adversaries survive negligence.

5. Architecture

5.1 The window

The reserve authority's sole operational function is to transact in unlimited quantity, in both directions, at a fixed nominal price.

  • Sell side. Any party delivering one basket unit of specification-grade commodities receives newly issued currency equal to the statutory basket price, less a spread of 0.5 percent.
  • Buy side. Any party surrendering currency equal to the statutory basket price, plus a spread of 0.5 percent, receives one basket unit, delivered as divisible tokenised warehouse warrants and claimable physically in full commercial lots.

No quantity target exists. The authority does not forecast, does not project, and does not decide how much currency should circulate.

Why this is countercyclical without a committee. In an expansion, commodity demand pushes market prices above the statutory price, so arbitrage flows toward buying commodities from the authority with currency. Currency is retired and the money supply contracts against the boom. In a contraction, commodity prices fall below the statutory price, arbitrage reverses, producers sell commodities to the authority for currency, and the money supply expands — delivering cash directly to producers in the sectors under the most severe stress.

This inverts the classical gold standard, under which the money supply was rigid and the entire burden of adjustment fell on wages and employment. It also inverts the original version of this proposal, which tied issuance to mining output and was therefore procyclical. Neither requires a vote.

5.2 Value-neutral rebalancing

A fixed-forever basket goes obsolete; a 1950 basket would be weighted toward coal and tin. A freely adjustable basket is monetary policy wearing a costume, and whoever sets the weights is the central bank. The resolution is to permit composition changes while making it arithmetically impossible for such changes to alter the currency's value.

$$\sum_i q_i^{\text{new}} \times p_i^{\text{ref}} \;=\; \sum_i q_i^{\text{old}} \times p_i^{\text{ref}}$$

Σ (new quantity of commodity i × reference price of i) = Σ (old quantity of commodity i × reference price of i)

Reference prices are the ninety-day volume-weighted average across a statutorily enumerated set of public exchanges, computed over a window that closes before the proposal is published. Using a trailing average rather than a spot date removes the incentive to time a rebalancing to a favourable print, which spot pricing would leave wide open. A rebalancing therefore changes what backs each currency unit and cannot change what a unit is worth or how many units exist.

Four procedural constraints apply. A proposed composition takes effect no earlier than twenty-four months after publication. Weight changes phase in linearly across the following twelve months. Adoption requires a legislative supermajority rather than administrative action. And no single cycle may alter more than a defined percentage of total basket value.

Every admitted commodity must be storable for years without material degradation, gradeable against an objective published specification, liquidly traded with continuous public price discovery, industrially essential rather than ornamental, and geographically diversified in production. The last criterion excludes cobalt and the rare earth elements regardless of their strategic importance. A currency whose backing can be cornered by controlling a small number of mines is not a currency; it is a single point of failure with a flag on it.

5.3 Blind adversarial audit

The oracle problem is the binding constraint on any commodity-backed system, and no cryptographic technique addresses it. A ledger verifies signatures. It cannot verify that physical goods exist. If the party holding the goods also controls the instruments that measure them, the system has reconstructed institutional trust and added latency. This is Thompson's trusting-trust problem relocated into a warehouse, and as Anderson observed of real cryptosystem failures, the breach will be organisational rather than mathematical.

The mitigation is therefore built as a game with an attacker rather than as a reporting pipeline.

  • Assignment. Auditors are drawn from a licensed, bonded, geographically distributed pool. Vault-to-auditor assignment is generated by publicly verifiable randomness under a commit-reveal scheme: the commitment is published in advance, but the assignment is revealed only at the moment of the auditor's arrival on site. The auditor does not know which vault they will inspect. Critically, the vault does not know which auditor will arrive, or when. One-sided blindness is insufficient, because a vault with advance notice of an inspection window can prepare for it.
  • Redundancy. Each audit event assigns two auditors barred from communicating. Each commits a cryptographic hash of their findings before either result is disclosed. Divergent results quarantine the lot pending a third assignment.
  • Incentives. Auditors post bonds as a condition of licensure. A bond is slashed where a later auditor discovers a discrepancy that a prior auditor certified clean. Discovery bounties are paid from slashed bonds. Catching fraud pays; missing it costs. This is a bug bounty with the economics inverted so that silence is expensive.
  • Standing to compel. Any warrant holder may trigger an unscheduled audit of any vault by posting a bond, returned with a bounty if a discrepancy is found and forfeited if none is. This grants adversarial parties, including short sellers, the standing that no existing reserve system provides. Adversaries with financial motive and legal access are the most reliable audit function ever devised, and every proof-of-reserve scheme to date has excluded them.
  • Redemption as the terminal oracle. Universal redemption is not a consumer convenience but the audit mechanism of last resort. Because the basket contains industrial inputs, the parties arbitraging any mispricing are smelters, mills and refiners who need the physical commodity to run their operations next quarter. They cannot be talked out of a delivery failure the way holders of a purely financial claim can. Gold redemption depends on speculators who want an asset. Basket redemption depends on manufacturers who need a material.

5.4 Credit: mark-to-market loan funds

Lending is removed from the monetary layer entirely. Credit is extended by independent loan funds that are equity-funded and marked to market. Because they carry no fixed redemption value, they cannot be run on; a panic moves a price rather than triggering a liquidity cascade.

This has a consequence for the circuit-breaker design in the original specification. A rule halting all lending on detection of an audit anomaly automates a bank run, since the anomaly and the panic arrive together. Under equity-funded credit, that coupling disappears. An audit anomaly quarantines the specific vault lot, deducts its contents from the attested basket, and the authority replaces the shortfall through the open window. The failure is local and never touches credit.

It also removes the mechanism identified by the Bank of England as the source of most money in circulation. Under this architecture, money is not created when a loan is written. It is issued against delivered physical goods. No one owes anyone for the existence of a dollar.

6. A Rejected Design: Cryptographic Hardware Inside Bullion

An earlier version of this specification proposed embedding piezoelectric micro-nodes inside bullion bars, harvesting energy from ultrasonic pulses to boot a cryptographic enclave and return a signed attestation. The underlying instinct about acoustics is sound and the mechanism is rejected anyway, for three reasons that generalise.

  • Fungibility. A bar containing electronics is no longer good-delivery bullion under recognised refining standards. It trades at a discount to the metal it contains, which defeats the purpose of holding it as a reserve.
  • Power budget. Piezoelectric harvesting through dense metal delivers microwatts. That is a hard ceiling well below what booting a secure enclave and performing asymmetric cryptography requires.
  • Attestation scope. This is the fatal one. The signature attests to the identity of a device, not to the composition of the metal surrounding it. An adversary capable of machining a bar is capable of transplanting a genuine node into a tungsten billet, producing a cryptographically valid attestation over a fraudulent bar. The system would not merely fail to detect the fraud; it would certify it.
The general lesson

Point three is the general lesson. Hardware attestation tells you what signed, never what is true. Any architecture in which a signature is treated as evidence about the physical world has substituted a measurement of the sensor for a measurement of the thing.

What replaces it. Verification is performed on the goods by instruments held outside the custodial boundary. For high-density metals, ultrasonic transit-time measurement is decisive. Gold and tungsten have almost identical densities, roughly 19.30 against 19.25 grams per cubic centimetre, which is precisely why tungsten is the canonical counterfeit core. Their acoustic velocities are not close: approximately 3,240 metres per second in gold against approximately 5,200 in tungsten. Continuous mass measurement under load cells in sealed vaults supplies an independent second channel, and geometric survey a third. Three independent physical channels, no embedded trust anchor, and instruments calibrated by a party with no custodial relationship to the metal.

Eleven of the twelve capabilities resolve to an outright denial. The twelfth is not a power but an arithmetic constraint. What remains is a warehouse with a published price list and a legal obligation to honour it in both directions.

8. Three Open Problems

The following are unsolved. They are stated as problems rather than concealed as caveats, and they are put to the reader deliberately. Any of the three could be dispositive, and a solution to any of the three would be a genuine contribution. Correspondence is invited.

Problem 1
The payments backstop

The discretionary interventions of 2008 and 2020 are unavailable to this authority by construction. Two of the three usual justifications for them fall away: the window is countercyclical automatically, and equity-funded loan funds cannot be run on. The third does not. A payments-system freeze arising from operational rather than solvency causes — a clearing failure, a cyber incident, a correspondent bank collapsing mid-settlement — has no backstop under this design. The obvious fixes reintroduce discretionary emergency lending, which is the capability the architecture exists to remove. The problem is to design a payments continuity facility whose activation conditions are objectively verifiable, whose capacity is bounded ex ante, and which cannot be repurposed as a general credit facility.

Problem 2
Valuation of illiquid credit

Mark-to-market discipline requires observable prices. A small-business loan, a commercial mortgage, and a project finance facility have none. Net asset value therefore becomes model-derived, and model-derived valuation is historically where accounting fraud concentrates. The architecture has moved the attack surface rather than eliminating it: instead of a bank misrepresenting its reserves, a loan fund misrepresents its marks. The problem is to specify a valuation and disclosure regime for non-traded credit that is adversarially auditable in the same sense as the vault layer. Note that this is substantially the same problem as the oracle problem, applied to a cash flow rather than a commodity, and a solution to one may inform the other.

Problem 3
Long-duration fixed-rate credit

The thirty-year fixed-rate mortgage exists in the United States primarily through federal guarantee. Under equity-funded, marked-to-market lending, interest rate risk shifts from intermediaries to savers. This is more honest and it is politically radioactive, since it removes the single most popular financial product in American life. The problem is to determine whether long-duration fixed-rate household credit can be provided under this architecture without reintroducing a guarantee, and if not, what instrument substitutes for it. Note that a proposal which reduces housing affordability will not be adopted regardless of its other merits, so this is a survival constraint rather than a refinement.

9. Who Holds the Key

The authority's charter is a statute. Congress created the Federal Reserve by statute in 1913 and could repeal that statute tomorrow; the same is true of anything proposed here. Its independence is statutory, not constitutional. No architecture binds a sovereign legislature, and any document claiming otherwise is describing a wish.

This has a direct implication for a related question. Litigation is not the available path. Taxpayers generally lack standing to challenge federal fiscal policy under Frothingham and its successors; the constitutionality of a national bank was settled in McCulloch v. Maryland and of fiat legal tender in the Legal Tender Cases; and federal debt is owed to third-party holders — pension funds, insurers, foreign central banks, the Social Security trust fund, individual savers — whose contracts no court can reassign to a party that is not the obligor. The door that is actually unlocked is legislative, and it requires a specification rather than a slogan.

The design objective, then, is not immutability. It is to make deviation slow, arithmetically visible, and attributable to named parties on the record. A discretionary system conceals its choices inside technical language and closed deliberation. A mechanical system forces anyone who wishes to change the currency's value to propose it in public, in writing, against a stated arithmetic constraint, and to vote for it under their own name.

That is the whole of the claim, and it is deliberately smaller than the slogan it replaces. This paper does not end central banking. It holds vaults, operates a window, and defines a unit of account, which are central banking functions and are not disguised here. What it removes is discretion. Unlike the slogan, that claim survives contact with a hostile reader — which, given the audience, is the only test that matters.
